## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original widescreen website hero background for a personal product designer portfolio. Create an evocative cinematic hand-painted anime landscape inspired by the celestial atmosphere of Kimi no Na wa (Your Name): an immense luminous cobalt and cyan summer sky, towering intricately painted sunlit cumulus clouds framing the sides, distant Japanese mountain ridges and a tiny lakeside town confined to the lowest 15 percent. A brilliant slim comet with a cyan-white trail and a delicate rose-pink companion streak sweeps diagonally through the upper right toward the low horizon, poetic feeling of distance and connection. Composition: panoramic 16:9, broad uninterrupted deep blue sky and subtle atmospheric texture across the left half to accommodate white website typography; detailed clouds and comet brightest on the right. A very narrow sunlit grass edge at the bottom. Sophisticated anime background painting, luminous natural light, finely rendered cloud edges, rich azure depth, emotional and quietly wondrous, not a 3D render. No people, no characters, no lettering, no typography, no logos, no movie branding, no watermark. This is original scenery, do not reproduce the poster composition.
