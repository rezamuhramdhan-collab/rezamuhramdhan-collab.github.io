About

Based in
Stockholm, working CET with overlap to US East
Tools
Figma, React, TypeScript, Rive, a lot of paper

Nine years of
unglamorous detail

I started out building what I drew, first for museums and then for a utility company where the users wore gloves and the interface had to work in the rain. That decade taught me to distrust anything that only looks right on a laptop.

Now I work on money movement and the systems underneath it. The work I am proudest of is usually invisible: a queue that stopped growing, a settlement flow that support no longer has to explain, a component library that outlived the team that wrote it.

I work best embedded in a small team with direct access to the people who use the thing. I write, I prototype in code, and I would rather show you a rough build on Thursday than a polished deck next month.
